Design Foundation / UI & UX System

The visual language of altitude & clarity.

Nimble Nimbus combines deep atmospheric blues, sharp 0px architectural geometry, high-impact display typography, and ethereal 3D WebGL motion.

Harmonious light & depth palette.

Horizon blue. Bottom gradient depth transition.

--display #8EC5FC

Soft sky blue. Primary display heading tone.

--accent #EAF2FD

Brilliant white. Two-tone <em> emphasis highlights.

--body #D4E5F9

Light periwinkle. High-readability editorial copy.

--subtext #5B8EC2

Muted steel blue. Metadata, dates & kicker labels.

1px hairline dividers & grid borders.

--wash rgba(142, 197, 252, 0.12)

Hover surfaces & interactive row highlights.

04 / Motion & Interaction

Living WebGL & scroll dynamics.

Three.js 3D Nimbus Cloud

Interactive GLTF 3D model with dual directional/hemisphere rim lighting, mouse-move parallax rotation, and scroll-bound altitude descent.

  • • Model: /nimbus-cloud.glb
  • • HemisphereLight: #EAF2FD / #113264 (2.5)
  • • DirectionalLight: #8EC5FC (3.0)
  • • Tilt Easing: 0.018 interpolation
Hero Rain Navigation

Animated menu labels descending from under the cloud in a continuous loop with randomized staggered delays [0s, 2s, 3s, 4s, 5s] and gentle sinusoidal wobble.

  • • Fall Duration: 22s - 25s
  • • Wobble Frequency: 9s ease-in-out
  • • Hover: Pauses animation & brightens to #8EC5FC
